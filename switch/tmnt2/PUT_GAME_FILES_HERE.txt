TMNT 2: Battle Nexus - Switch port

Copy the whole "switch" folder to the root of your SD card, then copy these
6 files from YOUR OWN PC copy of the game into this folder (sdmc:/switch/tmnt2/):

  TMNT.DAT
  TMNTE.DAT
  WaveData.bin
  SeData.bin
  TrSeData.bin
  (optional) voice0.bin ... voice3.bin, strbgm.vas  - voices and music, if your copy has them

TMNT2V00.DAT ... TMNT2V05.DAT here are empty placeholders for the cutscene movies.
If your copy has the real ones (several MB each), use those instead.

The game files are not included: they are Konami's copyrighted data.

Launch from the Homebrew Menu opened with title override
(hold R while starting any game) so the port gets full RAM.
Saves go to sdmc:/switch/tmnt2/ifs/, the log to sdmc:/switch/tmnt2/log.txt.
