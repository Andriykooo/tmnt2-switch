  custom GLES3/OpenAL/Joy-Con runtime. No game data is included. Copy TMNT.DAT, TMNTE.DAT,
  WaveData.bin, SeData.bin and TrSeData.bin from your own PC copy into /switch/tmnt2/.
  Beta: no movie playback; voices and music need voice0-3.bin and strbgm.vas.
